# TruckFood — Product Manifesto

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Last Update:** 2026-09-15

TruckFood is built for the reality of professional road transport.

A truck driver does not only need to know where a restaurant is. The driver needs to know whether the journey, the stop and the place itself make sense for the vehicle, the schedule and the human being behind the wheel.

TruckFood exists to turn scattered information into practical journey intelligence.

## What We Want to Solve

- A truck should not be sent blindly towards a road, bridge or access point that cannot accommodate it.
- A driver should be able to find a realistic place to stop and park.
- A driver should be able to find somewhere to eat without losing unnecessary time.
- A driver should be able to organise rest around the realities of professional driving.
- Community knowledge should become useful, structured and trustworthy data.

## What We Refuse to Build

We will not optimise the product around vanity metrics while ignoring driver safety and usefulness.

We will not present uncertain information as fact.

We will not make the product dependent on a single provider when a reasonable abstraction can protect the platform.

We will not allow uncontrolled feature growth to destroy reliability.

## The Experience

**Chegar. Parar. Comer. Descansar. Continuar.**

This sequence is the emotional and functional backbone of TruckFood.

## Long-Term Ambition

TruckFood can evolve into a global professional-driver platform covering routing intelligence, parking, food, rest, services, vehicle context, community knowledge and trusted journey information.

The ambition is large. The execution must remain disciplined.
