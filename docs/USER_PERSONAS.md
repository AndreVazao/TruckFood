# TruckFood — User Personas

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Discovery baseline

## Persona 1 — Professional Driver

### Goal
Complete the journey safely and efficiently while dealing with the real constraints of a heavy vehicle.

### Needs

- know whether an access or route is suitable;
- find a place to stop;
- find realistic truck parking;
- eat without unnecessary detours or delays;
- rest;
- know what other drivers have experienced;
- quickly distinguish trustworthy information from uncertain information.

### Pain Points

- unsuitable roads and access points;
- lack of parking;
- outdated opening hours;
- restaurants that cannot accommodate trucks;
- poor or incomplete local information;
- information spread across many apps and informal sources.

### Product Priority
**Highest.** This persona defines the product.

## Persona 2 — Owner-Driver / Independent Operator

Shares the driver's needs but may also care about vehicle operating costs, predictable stops and reliable journey planning.

## Persona 3 — Fleet Operator

Needs a broader operational view and may eventually require fleet-oriented tools, policies, saved vehicles, reporting and commercial controls.

This persona is important for the long-term platform but should not distort the first MVP.

## Persona 4 — Location / Restaurant Operator

Wants professional drivers to discover the business and needs accurate business information, truck access details and useful visibility.

Commercial features must not compromise driver trust.

## Persona 5 — Community Contributor

A driver or trusted user who contributes observations, corrections, photos, reviews and confirmations.

The system must make contribution useful without making it easy to poison critical data.

## Persona 6 — Administrator / Moderator

Responsible for data quality, moderation, abuse handling, source management and operational integrity.

## Priority Rule

When product interests conflict, the default order is:

1. driver safety and usefulness;
2. data integrity and trust;
3. operational reliability;
4. business value;
5. growth and engagement.
