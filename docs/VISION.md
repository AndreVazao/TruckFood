# TruckFood — Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Draft for Discovery  

## Vision

Create a trusted digital companion for professional truck drivers that understands the practical reality of moving a heavy vehicle across roads and borders.

TruckFood should help answer four fundamental questions:

- **Can I get there?**
- **Where can I stop?**
- **Where can I eat?**
- **Where can I rest?**

And then help the driver continue.

## The Driver Journey

### 1. CHEGAR

Provide information that can help identify potentially unsuitable roads, bridges, streets, access points and restrictions for the driver's vehicle.

Examples of relevant constraints include:

- height;
- weight;
- width;
- length;
- vehicle class;
- prohibited vehicle types;
- temporary restrictions;
- access restrictions;
- time-dependent restrictions.

The system must distinguish verified information from uncertain or inferred information.

### 2. PARAR

Help the driver find realistic stopping and parking opportunities, including information such as:

- truck suitability;
- capacity where known;
- availability signals where available;
- security;
- lighting;
- toilets;
- showers;
- cost;
- opening/access conditions;
- special suitability such as refrigerated transport or ADR where relevant.

### 3. COMER

Help drivers discover food options that fit professional transport realities:

- truck access;
- parking;
- opening hours;
- price level;
- service speed where known;
- community experience;
- useful driver information.

### 4. DESCANSAR

Support trip planning around legal, practical and human rest needs without pretending to provide legal or safety guarantees beyond the available data.

### 5. CONTINUAR

After the stop, the driver should be able to continue with better information about the next part of the journey.

## Product Strategy

TruckFood should initially complement existing navigation systems rather than attempting to replace every navigation capability.

The platform can become valuable by adding the missing professional-driver context around maps and routing.

## Long-Term Vision

Potential future capabilities include:

- truck-aware journey planning;
- parking intelligence;
- restaurant and service discovery;
- vehicle profiles;
- road restriction intelligence;
- community reporting;
- trusted journey facts;
- fleet-oriented tools;
- professional accounts;
- commercial partnerships;
- international support.

The long-term scope is intentionally separated from the first MVP.
